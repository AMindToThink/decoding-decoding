03 Two objects, two fits

The method has two parts, and — this is the correction to rev 1 — they have different supervision signals, both living on the LLM and text. Calibrating knobs on a toy HMM was a mistake on my part: the knob values are properties of how the model's inferred latent drifts on text (drift rate, per-token informativeness, forgetting timescale), and a toy I picked has no reason to share them.

  • The filter and its knobs — the latent dynamics. Supervised by the imprint trajectories you are already measuring. No involved.
  • The map — turning the latent into the per-candidate reweighting. Supervised by soft-Bellman self-consistency. No labels involved either.

The knobs are a parameterization of the in-context inference, so fitting them on text and characterizing the imprint are the same measurement.

05 Fitting the map, also without

This is where I was too pessimistic last turn. The soft-Bellman recursion lets bootstrap. Taking logs of and substituting the ansatz on both sides gives a residual

and you fit by minimizing over text prefixes, semi-gradient (stop-grad on the bootstrap target), exactly as in soft Q-learning. The inner sum uses the model's logits from one forward pass; the terminal anchor fixes the scale. No ground-truth is ever observed. This is the same squared-residual objective Twisted SMC uses for its twist, specialized to a log-linear-in-latent value class.

Targets, ranked by how much they cost

(A) Bootstrapped soft-Bellman residual — cheap, -free, the default. (B) Monte-Carlo rollouts — roll out continuations, form importance-weighted estimates of , regress onto them; direct but high-variance, the thing SMC resampling fights. Use as a spot-check on (A). (C) Distillation — run an expensive-but-correct sampler (TSMC, LHTS, or short-horizon exact DP) as a teacher on text prefixes, and regress the student (knobs ) onto the teacher's relative twist . Targets the only thing that matters — the cross-candidate shape — directly.

06 What the toy is actually for

Not calibration. Its single legitimate role is a one-time method unit-test: in a setting with clean ground-truth (exact by DP) and a latent that is sufficient by construction (Xie-style mixture-of-HMMs), can this estimator class fit the map at all? If the log-linear twist can't recover even there, the functional form is wrong and you've avoided debugging it blind on the LLM where is invisible. That is an existence proof, not a transfer of numbers. The one way the toy could inform values — first fitting the toy's latent dynamics to the LLM's measured imprint — buys nothing, because by then you have measured the LLM and can fit the knobs directly.

07 Regime and scope

  • Mild tempering is friendly. As , becomes max-dominated (the collapses toward a max), the value-to-go gets spiky, and a smooth latent estimator is weakest — the same regime where the gap matters most. Expect graceful degradation, and expect the Bellman residual to be hardest to drive down there.
  • Scalar latent is defensible for temperature specifically. For blacklist or frequency-penalty decoders, needs token-identity information a scalar cannot carry, so the method is temperature-shaped by design. Match estimator dimensionality to the decoder's latent dimensionality; that mapping is a clean statement of which decoders the method can touch.

08 Why this is the right shape

The estimator is a model of the in-context inference, so the corrector and the imprint characterization become one object. Two things follow that a generic neural twist would not give you:

  • The Bellman-residual floor — how far below zero you cannot push with a log-linear-in-latent class — is a direct measurement of how non-low-dimensional the imprint is. The thing that limits the method also quantifies the phenomenon. That is Experiment B answered from the other side.
  • If the per-token observation has an exponential-family likelihood for the latent, the streaming update is conjugate and closed-form, which is what makes the per-candidate evaluation in §02 cheap rather than prohibitive.
The honest bar

Not "match ." It is "beat by a measurable margin with three interpretable knobs and one coefficient vector ." Low floor for a first result; high ceiling if the latent really is sufficient — and the residual floor tells you which case you are in.

Both fits run on the LLM, on text. The toy is a form-check you run once. Ground-truth never appears except as an optional Monte-Carlo audit of the bootstrap.
